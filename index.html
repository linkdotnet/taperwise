Plan

Built around you.
Adjusted every morning.

A plan that starts where you are.

Taperwise reads your running history from Apple Health, or imports GPX, TCX, FIT and Strava exports. Then it builds a 12 to 30 week plan for 5K, 10K, half marathon or marathon: Base, Build, Peak and Taper. Your goal, today’s fitness and an honest race-day range sit side by side.

  • Volume grows at most about 10% a week
  • Every fourth week is a recovery week
  • Long runs grow at most 10%, capped at 35 km or 3.5 hours

Every session, on a calendar.

See the plan week by week or month by month. Filled days are runs you did, coloured dots are what comes next. Tap a session for its steps, target paces, heart rate zone and, on long runs, a drink and gel plan.

Listens to your body.

Heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep and training load decide whether today is green, yellow or red. A yellow morning trims the session, three red days bring a deload, and missed runs are never made up. Sore legs or a cold? Tell Taperwise and the plan pauses, then eases you back in.

Strength that keeps you running.

Two short sessions a week of split squats, calf raises and step-ups, the moves linked to fewer running injuries.1 A guided circuit counts sets and rest on iPhone or Apple Watch and saves to Apple Health.

Live on iPhone

Big numbers.
Clear targets.

Your iPhone records the run on its own, no watch needed. Pace sits big and white over the map with a gauge for the target, interval steps count down, and spoken cues tell you when to push and when to jog. Pause, End and Get me home stay in thumb reach.

  • Drift cue. Heart rate creeping up at the same effort? Drink, take a gel or ease off.
  • Fluid estimate. From your sweat rate, with a nudge before you lose 2% of body mass.
  • Long-run guard. A heads-up when today’s run passes your longest of the last 30 days by 10%.

Race Pilot

Race day,
planned to the second.

Set a goal time, or let your training predict one,2 and Taperwise turns it into a pace band for every 5 km. Import the course GPX and each kilometre adapts to the climbs.

  • Carb load and race morning. How many grams a day for the two days before, and what to eat before the start.
  • Gels by kilometre. Spread through the race, with a reminder on your wrist.
  • Ahead or behind. Live against your plan, so the first kilometre stays slow.

Insights

Watch your fitness grow.

  1. Fitness trend

    Your VDOT week by week, with estimates for 10K, half and marathon and a race-day range.

  2. Recovery

    Overnight HRV, resting heart rate and sleep against your usual range, with the reasons spelled out.

  3. Training load

    Fitness, fatigue and form, plus the acute to chronic load ratio.

  4. Weekly volume

    Kilometres every week, the long run and the rest of the week, and how often you run.

  5. Easy vs. hard

    How close you stay to 80% easy running.

  6. Running form

    Cadence, ground contact, stride length, vertical oscillation and power, week by week.

  7. Aerobic efficiency

    Pace at the same heart rate over months, so you see the engine grow.

  8. Consistency

    Every day of the last 26 weeks on one grid.

  9. Monotony & strain

    Same load every day raises the risk. Vary hard and easy days.

Apple Health

Built on Apple Health.
Set up once.

Taperwise reads and writes Apple Health, so your zones, effort and workouts match what Fitness and the Workout app show. Nothing to configure twice, nothing stored anywhere else.

  • Your heart rate zones. On iOS 27, Taperwise uses the zones you set in Apple Health, or Apple’s automatic ones.4 Prefer Taperwise’s own? They are written into every workout, so Fitness shows the same.
  • Overnight HRV. Readiness uses RMSSD from your Apple Watch’s beat-to-beat readings while you sleep, against your own usual range.3
  • Elevation from the barometer. iPhone and Apple Watch measure climbs with the barometer, levelled once to GPS, for a steadier grade-adjusted pace.
  • Shared only recent data? Recovery and Training load say so and tell you how long a full baseline takes.

Apple Intelligence

A coach that never leaves your iPhone.

With Apple Intelligence turned on, Taperwise writes short notes on today’s session, your week and every run. Describe your goal in a sentence and it fills in the plan for you to check.

  • On-device only. Notes come from Apple’s on-device model. No server, no cloud model, no data sent.
  • Only when you opt in. If Apple Intelligence is off, or you switch notes off in Settings, the feature stays hidden.
  • Rules decide, AI explains. The training rules make every decision. The notes never change your plan.

Just you and your run.

No feed, no followers, no leaderboards. Taperwise was built without a server on purpose. Health data stays in Apple Health, your plan stays on your iPhone and coaching runs on-device. The only thing the app downloads is map tiles.

  • No servers. Nothing to sync, nothing to breach, nothing to shut down.
  • No community. No feed to keep up with, no kudos to chase. The only runner you race is last month’s you.
