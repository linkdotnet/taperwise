Plan

### Built around you. Adjusted every morning.

#### A plan that starts where you are.

Taperwise reads your running history from Apple Health, or imports GPX, TCX, FIT and Strava exports. Then it builds a 12 to 30 week plan for 5K, 10K, half marathon or marathon: Base, Build, Peak and Taper. Your goal, today’s fitness and an honest race-day range sit side by side.

- Volume grows at most about 10% a week

- Every fourth week is a recovery week

- Long runs grow at most 10%, capped at 35 km or 3.5 hours

#### Every session, on a calendar.

See the plan week by week or month by month. Filled days are runs you did, coloured dots are what comes next. Tap a session for its steps, target paces, heart rate zone and, on long runs, a drink and gel plan.

#### Listens to your body.

Heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep and training load decide whether today is green, yellow or red. A yellow morning trims the session, three red days bring a deload, and missed runs are never made up. Sore legs or a cold? Tell Taperwise and the plan pauses, then eases you back in.

#### Strength that keeps you running.

Two short sessions a week of split squats, calf raises and step-ups, the moves linked to fewer running injuries. A guided circuit counts sets and rest on iPhone or Apple Watch and saves to Apple Health.

Live on iPhone

### Big numbers. Clear targets.

Your iPhone records the run on its own, no watch needed. Pace sits big and white over the map with a gauge for the target, interval steps count down, and spoken cues tell you when to push and when to jog. Pause, End and Get me home stay in thumb reach.

- **Drift cue.** Heart rate creeping up at the same effort? Drink, take a gel or ease off.

- **Fluid estimate.** From your sweat rate, with a nudge before you lose 2% of body mass.

- **Long-run guard.** A heads-up when today’s run passes your longest of the last 30 days by 10%.

Race Pilot

### Race day, planned to the second.

Set a goal time, or let your training predict one, and Taperwise turns it into a pace band for every 5 km. Import the course GPX and each kilometre adapts to the climbs.

- **Carb load and race morning.** How many grams a day for the two days before, and what to eat before the start.

- **Gels by kilometre.** Spread through the race, with a reminder on your wrist.

- **Ahead or behind.** Live against your plan, so the first kilometre stays slow.

#### Works without signal.

Download the areas you run in, from overview to street detail. On Wi-Fi, Taperwise keeps the places you run available offline automatically, and the maps you have looked at stay in a 200 MB cache. Send a detailed area to your watch and leave the phone at home.

Apple Watch

### Leave the phone at home.

The watch runs the workout itself, with no phone and no network. Pages laid out like the Workout app: metrics, intervals, zones, chart, elevation, fuel and Now Playing. Pause with the Action button, and do your strength circuit right on your wrist.

After the run

Heart strain, cadence, estimated VO₂max, grade-adjusted pace and decoupling for every run. Scrub through pace, heart rate and elevation and watch the spot light up on the map.

Apple Intelligence

### A coach that never leaves your iPhone.

With Apple Intelligence turned on, Taperwise writes short notes on today’s session, your week and every run. Describe your goal in a sentence and it fills in the plan for you to check.

- **On-device only.** Notes come from Apple’s on-device model. No server, no cloud model, no data sent.

- **Only when you opt in.** If Apple Intelligence is off, or you switch notes off in Settings, the feature stays hidden.

- **Rules decide, AI explains.** The training rules make every decision. The notes never change your plan.

And more

### Everything a race build needs.

#### Honest race prediction

~3:34

Your marathon estimate blends race fitness with the training you actually put in, shown as a race-day range with an honest “on track”, “ambitious” or “unrealistic”.

#### Aerobic decoupling

3.2%

Heart-rate drift in the second half of steady runs. Under 5% three times in a row unlocks the next long-run step.

#### Your history, in one place

Runs from Apple Health and your imports, by month, on a calendar or on one map, with splits, zones and best efforts.

#### Heart strain

7.9

How hard every run was on a scale from 0 to 21: light, moderate, high or all out.

#### Hydration and fuel

Weigh in before and after to learn your sweat rate. Drink and gel plans for long runs and race day, and a fluid estimate while you run.

#### Your run screen

Pick the fields, order the pages and see a live preview for iPhone and Apple Watch.

#### Rewards for consistency

Plan streaks, session points, a long-run ladder and badges for your first 30 km or a full taper. Extra kilometres earn nothing, and you only compete with your past self.

#### Bring your history

Apple Health, GPX, TCX, FIT and whole Strava exports, with duplicates detected automatically.

#### Body check

Rate every run from 1 to 10, saved to Apple Health as effort. Pain two days running? The plan offers a pause and brings you back at 60%.

#### Get me home

One tap shows an arrow and the distance back to your start, on iPhone and Apple Watch, even offline.

#### Live Activity

Pace, distance, heart rate and what’s left on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

### Just you and your run.

No feed, no followers, no leaderboards. Taperwise was built without a server on purpose. Health data stays in Apple Health, your plan stays on your iPhone and coaching runs on-device. The only thing the app downloads is map tiles.

- **No account.** Open the app and start. No sign-up, no password, no email.

- **No servers.** Nothing to sync, nothing to breach, nothing to shut down.

- **No community.** No feed to keep up with, no kudos to chase. The only runner you race is last month’s you.

- **No tracking.** No analytics, no ads and no third-party SDKs.
